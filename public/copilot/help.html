Beginner guide

Ten minutes to get set up, then one feature at a time. Tick the boxes as you go (saved on this device).

1 · Set up (once)

  • Open Context & documents → New profile. Fill in who you are, your company, products, prices you may share, tone and your playbook.
  • Upload 1–3 documents you use with customers (price list, catalogue, company deck). PDF, DOCX, TXT or MD.
  • Add one real contact in Contacts with tags and a birthday or follow-up date.
  • On first use, allow the microphone (and screen sharing on a computer) when the browser asks.
Example context: “I'm Tammy, sales director at ACME Beauty (Bangkok). We sell vitamin C serum (wholesale 990 THB, MOQ 50) and sunscreen. Goal: get distributors to order a trial. Tone: warm, polite, concise; use ค่ะ in Thai. Never promise exclusivity or delivery under 7 days. Objection ‘too expensive’ → explain the 30-day sell-through guarantee.”

2 · Online meeting (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams)

  1. Join your meeting as usual. On a computer, the meeting can be in the Zoom app or in a browser tab.
  2. Open New session → choose Online meeting, your context, documents and the contact.
  3. Click Start. Allow the microphone, then pick what to share:
    • Meeting in a browser tab → choose that tab and tick Share tab audio.
    • Zoom/Teams app on Windows → choose Entire screen and tick Share system audio.
    • Zoom app on Mac → use Zoom in the browser instead (macOS cannot share app audio).
  4. Watch the three panels: 1 what is said (translated), 2 what they want, 3 what you can say.
  5. Click ■ Stop at the end. The summary and next steps are saved in History.
Privacy tip: this copilot is a website, so it cannot hide itself from screen sharing. In the meeting app, share only the window or tab you want people to see — never your entire screen with the copilot open.

3 · In person — lunch, office, events (phone or computer)

  1. On your phone, open the copilot and log in (add it to your home screen for one-tap access).
  2. New session → In person. Pick your language and theirs (or Auto-detect).
  3. Press Start and allow the microphone. Put the phone on the table, screen up, and keep the page open.
  4. Swipe between the tabs 1 · Transcript, 2 · They ask, 3 · Replies.

One microphone hears both of you, so the copilot guesses who is speaking: lines show ME?, THEM? or VOICE. If the phone locks or you switch apps, listening pauses — come back and tap Resume audio capture.

Example: at lunch the customer asks in Thai “ส่งราคาส่งให้ได้ไหมคะ”. Panel 1 shows the English translation, panel 2 adds “Send wholesale price list” to the checklist, panel 3 suggests “ได้ค่ะ เดี๋ยวส่งให้ทาง LINE วันนี้เลยค่ะ” with its English meaning.

4 · Listen in your earphones, pop-out window, second device

  • Earphones: under the panels choose Read panel 1, 2 or 3. Only new content is read. Use ▶ play, ❚❚ pause, ↺ replay, 🔇 mute.
  • Pop out (computer, Chrome/Edge): a small window that stays on top of Zoom or your email. Drag it anywhere; ✕ hides it, Pop out brings it back.
  • 📱 Other device: shows a link and QR code. Open it on your phone or tablet to follow the panels and hear them there while the computer listens.
  • Bluetooth glasses / earphones (e.g. Ray-Ban Meta): connect them to the phone first; then they work as microphone and speaker like any headset. Pick them under Microphone if listed.

5 · Read my screen

  1. Open the email, document, slide, image or chart you want help with.
  2. In Reply assistant click 📷 Read my screen and choose that window. During a session use 📷 Read screen in the top bar or the 📷 tab of the pop-out.
  3. You get the text, a translation, a summary and draft replies with Copy buttons. Nothing is sent.

On a phone, the button takes a photo or lets you pick a screenshot instead (phones don't allow websites to capture the screen).

6 · Replies for LINE, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email…

  1. Copy the conversation from the app (or use Read my screen).
  2. Paste it in Reply assistant, pick the channel and the contact, add a note like “decline politely”.
  3. Choose one of the 2–3 drafts → Copy → paste in the app and send it yourself.

Personal LINE and LinkedIn accounts have no API, so the copilot never reads or sends messages there by itself.

7 · Contacts, reminders and greetings

  • Add tags (journalist, KOL, new/existing customer…), channels, preferred language and tone, notes and a follow-up date.
  • Add events: birthday, wedding, illness, promotion or your own.
  • Upcoming lists the next 14 days with a ready message for each; the group helper writes one personal message per contact for a tag.

8 · Morning summary

  1. In Morning summary add your brands, products and keywords, then Save.
  2. It runs automatically the first time you open the copilot each day (or click Run now).
  3. Each item has a source link and date; “nothing found” is said clearly. Social networks are best-effort.
